Dew Point Weather Widgets

JustDew

A tiny weather app for the number that actually tells you how sticky it feels.

JustDew keeps dew point visible on iPhone, Mac, and Apple Watch. Choose your current location or save a city, then keep the latest dew point on your Home Screen, Mac desktop, Lock Screen, and watch face.

Designed around one useful number

Dew Point at a Glance

Open the app for the current dew point, air temperature, humidity, location, and last update time.

Home Screen Widgets

Small and medium widgets show dew point clearly, with compact air and humidity context.

Mac Desktop Widgets

Keep dew point visible on macOS with native widget styling that respects the desktop appearance.

Watch Complications

Apple Watch complications include circular and corner layouts for fast face-level checks.

Grossness Scale

A blue to yellow to red scale makes mild, muggy, and Miami-style dew points easy to scan.

Current or Saved Location

Follow your current location automatically, or save a city and keep widgets pinned to that place.

Why dew point?

Humidity alone can be misleading. Dew point is a steadier way to understand how much moisture is actually in the air, which makes it a better quick read for comfort.

Built for widgets first

The app exists to configure location and refresh weather. The main experience is the widget or watch complication you see throughout the day.
