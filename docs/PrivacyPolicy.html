Privacy Policy

No tracking. No analytics. No developer-side location logs.

JustDew uses location only to show dew point and weather forecasts for the place you select. The developer does not collect, sell, share, or log your location, weather data, identifiers, or app usage.

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Core Principles

  • No analytics or tracking: JustDew does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • No developer servers: We do not operate servers that receive or store your location or weather data.
  • Local state: The app saves your locations, weather readings and forecasts, and comfort preferences locally on your device. Widgets and watch complications use the weather cache.

Location

If you choose Current Location, JustDew asks for system location permission. Your location is used to request weather and dew point forecasts and to show a readable place name such as city and state.

If you search for a city or address, JustDew uses that selected place to retrieve weather. The saved place is stored locally on your device for the app, widgets, and watch complications.

Weather and Apple Services

JustDew uses Apple system services, including WeatherKit and location/geocoding services, to return weather and place information. JustDew does not use those requests to build user profiles, run analytics, or maintain developer-side logs.

What JustDew Does Not Collect

  • It does not collect your precise or approximate location for developer-side storage.
  • It does not collect app usage analytics.
  • It does not collect advertising identifiers.
  • It does not sell or share personal data.
  • It does not require an account.

Local Data

JustDew stores saved locations, weather readings and forecasts, and your dew point comfort limit locally. The app and its widget extensions on the same device share a weather cache. Saved locations are separate on each device. You can change or remove saved locations in the app. These features do not send data to developer servers.

Children's Privacy

JustDew does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a material way, the updated policy will be published on this page and the date above will be changed.

Contact
